Privacy policy

Effective: on Harmonling 1.0's release · Applies to: Harmonling for Mac, iPad and iPhone

Harmonling collects nothing. It runs on your device, asks for no permissions, and the paths you save travel only through your own iCloud account. This page says what the app uses, and what for.

What Harmonling collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Harmonling makes no network requests of its own. There is no server of ours to send anything to.

Your settings (the key, layout and chords you last chose, what Show displays, the instrument, your place in Learn and your Train level) are saved with the system’s standard preferences storage, inside the app’s own container on your device. They never leave it, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Your saved paths

When you tap Save Path, Harmonling keeps the path, its key and its chords in order, on your device and in your own iCloud key-value storage, so it shows up in the Library on your other Macs, iPads and iPhones signed in to the same Apple Account. That storage belongs to your iCloud account: Apple keeps it for you, and we have no access to it.

If iCloud is off, your paths stay on the device. Delete a path in the Library and it is deleted on your other devices too.

Permissions

None. Harmonling does not use the microphone, the camera, Bluetooth, MIDI devices, your location, your contacts or your photos, and it reads no files.

Purchases

Harmonling Pro and its free 14-day trial are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or address. Harmonling learns only whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

What Harmonling does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and nothing to sell or share.

Harmonling collects no personal information from anyone, children under 13 included. Its instruments and drum sounds are open-source material that runs on your device and sends nothing; they are credited in the app’s About screen.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Harmonling: fretlingapp@gmail.com. If Harmonling’s data behavior ever changes, this policy changes before the app does.
