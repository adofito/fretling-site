The four places

Explore, Library, Learn and Train. On a Mac they are the control at the top of the window; on iPad and iPhone, the tab bar.

The map

The chord in the middle is home. Tap any chord to hear it: an arrow shows where it usually goes, and a dashed line joins it to the chords it shares two notes with. The panel beside the map plays it, holds it, arpeggiates it and lists where it usually goes next.

Function and Fifths

Function groups the key by what each chord does: Home, Away and Tension. Fifths lays the same chords around the circle of fifths. Both are free.

Write and play a path

Tap Write, then tap chords in order: each one joins the path. Play hears it through, and Voices shows how each note moves while it plays. Save Path keeps it in the Library.

Show: sevenths and satellites

Triads or Sevenths, and the satellites around the key: V7 of each chord is free; ii of, tritone substitutes, borrowed chords and Borrow from, vii°7 of, the Neapolitan and the mediants are Pro. A locked button wears a lock and opens the unlock sheet. Pro

Make This Home and the key

Make This Home redraws the map around the chord you picked. The key menu beside the title picks any key, major or natural minor free, the modes and the other minors with Pro. Pro

Library

Your saved paths, in your own iCloud storage, so a path saved on the Mac opens on the iPad. Open one to hear it on the map again. Three are free; Pro keeps up to 500, and a path you saved never locks. Pro

Learn

Ten lessons, each a page that asks you to play the chord it names on the map, from home and its neighbors to borrowed chords and modes. The first two are free. Pro

Train

A short progression plays in Explore’s key; name each chord you hear. Change the key in Explore and Train follows it, and your level stays where you left it. Levels 1 to 4 are free. Pro

The tours

The first time you open Explore, a short tour asks you to tap the glowing V and follow its arrow home. On a Mac, Help › Show Tour brings it back.
