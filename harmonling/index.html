Where a chord goes

Tap a chord to hear it. Arrows show where it usually goes, dashes the chords it shares two notes with. The Function layout groups the key as Home, Away and Tension; Fifths lays it around the circle.

Major and natural minor, the triads and the secondary dominants are free.

Write a path, hear it

Tap Write, then tap chords in order: each one joins the path. Play it back, and Voices shows how each note moves from chord to chord.

Save Path keeps it in your Library, on every device signed into your Apple Account. Three saved paths are free; Pro keeps up to 500. Pro

Any chord can be home

Pick a chord and Make This Home: the map redraws around it, so you can hear where the same chords lead from a new center.

The modes, Dorian to Locrian, and harmonic and melodic minor are Pro. Pro

Chords from outside the key

Under Show, the satellites join the map: secondary dominants (free), and with Pro the ii of each chord, tritone substitutes, borrowed chords with Borrow from, vii°7 of, the Neapolitan and the mediants. Sevenths turns every chord into its seventh. Pro

Ten lessons, played

Each lesson is a page that asks you to play the chord it names, on the map. The course fills in as you go, from home and its neighbors to modes.

The first two lessons are free. Pro

Name it by ear

A progression plays in Explore’s key. Name each chord you hear, and the levels climb from home and its fifth to the whole key.

Levels 1 to 4 are free. Pro
