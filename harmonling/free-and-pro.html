Free and Pro

The map, a path and the ear for the key you are in are free, for good. Harmonling Pro is the deep end: bought once, owned forever, on every device you sign into.

On this page

  • What's free
  • What Pro adds
  • The 14-day trial
  • How unlocking works

What's free

The free app is not a trial, and nothing in it expires.

  • The map in every key, major and natural minor, with both layouts, Function and Fifths.
  • Triads, and the secondary dominants (V7 of each chord).
  • Every play control: Play, Hold, Arpeggio, Make This Home.
  • Write, Voices and three saved paths in the Library, on every device.
  • The first two lessons.
  • Train's Harmony levels 1 to 4.

Nothing that is free today moves behind the unlock later.

The 14-day trial

Try all of Pro free for two weeks, from the button on the unlock sheet. Nothing starts on its own, nothing renews, and when it ends Harmonling returns to the free tier with your paths and settings where you left them.

How unlocking works

  • One purchase, once. No subscription, and no tip jar.
  • Every device. The unlock covers every Mac, iPad and iPhone signed into your Apple Account, and supports Family Sharing.
  • Restore Purchases is on the unlock sheet, for a new device or a reinstall.
