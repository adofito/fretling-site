Fingered, for both hands

Every scale and arpeggio carries its finger numbers. Pick the right hand, the left or both, and each key shows the finger that plays it.

Major, Minor, both Pentatonics, Blues, and Harmonic and Melodic Minor are free. The modes, Dorian onward, are Pro. Pro

Every chord, every inversion

The chords of your key, in root position or any inversion, close or open, over the bass you pick. The Chord Atlas brings every quality without leaving the key.

The jazz voicings, shell, rootless and two-hand, are Pro. Pro

Play it, name it

Play a chord on a MIDI keyboard, or tap it on the keys on screen, and Pianoling names it, gives its numeral in your key, and lists the other readings. Both are free.

Or play it on an acoustic piano and let Pianoling listen through the microphone. Best on simple chords, and on single notes in the middle of the keyboard. Pro

Arpeggios, step by step

Five steps for every arpeggio. Hear it and Find it are free. Play it, In time and Blind grade what you play, over MIDI or by the microphone, in one key or around the cycle. Pro

The chords that hold a note

“I want to identify chords with E in C Major.” Every chord in your key that holds the note, from triads up to thirteenths, with the note's role in each.

Practice them with Hear it, Find it and Name it, free. Play it grades you. Pro

Sound and practice

  • The grand piano: an acoustic grand that rings with the resonance of its own strings.
  • The mixer: balance the notes, the chords and the metronome.
  • The metronome, complete: tempo, meter, subdivisions, tap and its sounds.
  • The drums: the step sequencer and its pattern library. Pro
  • Undo and Redo for your edits, with ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z on a keyboard.

Private by default

Pianoling runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. What you play on a MIDI keyboard stays on your device, and Bluetooth is used only to connect the keyboard you pair.

The microphone is used only by Live Detect, which graded practice listens through when no MIDI keyboard is connected. Audio is analyzed as it arrives and is never recorded, stored or sent. The privacy policy
