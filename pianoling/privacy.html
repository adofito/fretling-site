Privacy policy

Effective: on Pianoling 1.0's release · Applies to: Pianoling for Mac, iPad and iPhone

Pianoling collects nothing. It runs entirely on your device: what you play, on the screen, on a MIDI keyboard or into the microphone, stays there. This page says what the app uses, and what for.

What Pianoling collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Pianoling makes no network requests of its own. There is no server of ours to send anything to.

Your settings and practice preferences are saved with the system's standard preferences storage, inside the app's own container on your device. They never leave it, and they are removed when you delete the app.

MIDI and Bluetooth

When you connect a MIDI keyboard, over USB or Bluetooth, Pianoling reads the notes you play to light the keys, name chords and check a graded step. They are used live, on your device, and are never recorded, stored or sent.

Pianoling asks for Bluetooth only when you choose Bluetooth MIDI… to pair a keyboard, and uses it only to connect that keyboard. A USB keyboard needs no permission.

The microphone

Pianoling asks for the microphone for Live Detect (Pianoling Pro), which listens to an acoustic piano and names the notes and chords you play. The graded practice steps listen through Live Detect when no MIDI keyboard is connected.

Audio is analyzed live, on your device, only while Live Detect is listening. It is never recorded, stored or sent. Turn Live Detect off and capture stops; on iPhone and iPad it also stops while Pianoling is in the background.

Purchases

Pianoling Pro and its free 14-day trial are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or address. Pianoling learns only whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

What Pianoling does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and nothing to sell or share.

Pianoling collects no personal information from anyone, children under 13 included. Its instruments and drum sounds are open-source material that runs on your device and sends nothing; they are credited in the app's About screen.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Pianoling: fretlingapp@gmail.com. If Pianoling's data behavior ever changes, this policy changes before the app does.
