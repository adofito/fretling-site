The Pianoling guide

Everything Pianoling does, in the order you will meet it: the sentence, the keys, the hands, identifying, the arpeggios and the sound.

The sentence

One sentence runs across the top of the screen: I want to learn scales in C Major. Every colored word is a menu. Change a word and the keys redraw.

  • Learn: Scales, Chords, Triads and Arpeggios.
  • Identify: a chord, or the chords that hold a note.

The keys

Tap any key to hear it, slide across them, or hold several at once for a chord. The strip above the keys shows the octaves in view: drag it, or its ends, to move or widen the view. The bar above the strip sets the octaves, the labels, the shapes and the colors on every key.

A scale plays back from the play button beside its row of notes.

Hands and fingering

Under the keys, Hands picks the right hand, the left or both, and Fingering puts the finger that plays each key over it. With both hands, the left hand plays the octave below the right; on the key they share, the left hand's number sits on the left.

Chords and voicings

Pick a chord of your key in the sentence, then an inversion, or any bass of your own. Close and open voicings are free. Shell, rootless and two-hand voicings are Pro. Pro

The Chord Atlas, from the sentence, holds every root and quality: hear a chord, see it land on the keys, and read its Roman numeral in your key.

Identify

Tap a chord onto the keys, or play it on a MIDI keyboard, and Pianoling names it in your key and lists the other readings. Undo, Redo and Clear sit under the keys while you build it.

Chords with a note lists every chord in your key that holds it, with the note's role in each. Practice them with Hear it, Find it and Name it. Play it grades you. Pro

Live Detect listens through the microphone instead. Best on simple chords, and on single notes in the middle of the keyboard. Pro

A MIDI keyboard

Plug in a USB keyboard, or pair a Bluetooth one from the MIDI lamp at the top of the window. The keys you play light up on screen, and Identify names what you hold. MIDI input is free.

Arpeggios

  • Hear it and Find it: free.
  • Play it, In time and Blind: graded, over MIDI or by the microphone, in one key or around the cycle. Pro

Sound and practice

A grand piano that rings with the resonance of its own strings, a mixer, and the metronome with every meter and subdivision. The drums and their sequencer are Pro. Pro

Undo and Redo step back through your edits, with ⌘Z and ⇧⌘Z on a keyboard.
