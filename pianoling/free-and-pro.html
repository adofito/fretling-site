Free and Pro

Everything you need to learn the keys is free, for good. Pianoling Pro is the deep end on top: bought once, owned forever, on every device you sign into.

On this page

  • What's free
  • What Pro adds
  • The 14-day trial
  • How unlocking works

What's free

The free app is not a trial, and nothing in it expires.

  • Learn: Scales, Chords, Triads and Arpeggios, and the sentence with its two verbs.
  • Scales: Major, Minor, both Pentatonics, Blues, Harmonic Minor and Melodic Minor.
  • Chords: the Chord Atlas, with the chords of the key, Roman numerals and borrowed harmony.
  • On the keys: fingering for each hand, inversions, close and open voicings, and the labels, shapes and colors.
  • Arpeggios: Hear it and Find it on every arpeggio.
  • Identify: naming a chord tapped on screen or played on a MIDI keyboard, USB or Bluetooth.
  • Chords with a note: every chord set, and Hear it, Find it and Name it.
  • Sound and practice: the grand piano, the mixer and the complete metronome.

Nothing that is free today moves behind the unlock later.

The 14-day trial

Try all of Pro free for two weeks, from the button on the unlock sheet. Nothing starts on its own and nothing renews. When it ends, Pianoling returns to the free tier, and every choice you made comes back the moment you buy Pro.

How unlocking works

  • One purchase, once. No subscription.
  • Every device. Every Mac, iPad and iPhone signed into your Apple Account, with Family Sharing.
  • Restore anytime from the unlock sheet.
