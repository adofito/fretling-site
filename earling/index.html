A voice brought forward

A held chord brings each voice forward in turn while its name shows: the root, the 3rd, the 5th. Then one comes forward with no name, and you say which. Level by level the help fades, from a solo to no boost at all.

Spotlight's first level is free; the fading help, close and inverted chords and 7th chords are Pro. Pro

The note that went missing

The chord plays whole, then again with one voice gone. Find the gap on the chord, then sing the note that filled it.

Missing tone's first level, on open triads, is free. Inverted chords, 7th chords and two tones missing are Pro. Pro

Then sing it

Every round ends on Sing. The microphone draws your voice as a trace and holds you to the note, in whatever octave suits you, for about half a second. Headphones help; on speakers, the chord steps aside while you sing.

No microphone? Sing becomes a tap, and nothing else changes. Late at night, turn Sing off in Settings. Sing is free in every mode.

Eight modes, one path

From two notes to chords in a key: Higher or lower, Intervals, Chord quality, Spotlight, Missing tone, Scales and modes, Scale degrees and Chords in context. Each mode opens the next.

Higher or lower, Intervals and Chord quality are free at every level, and every other mode's first level is free too: 25 of the 47. Pro

See it three ways, in a key

Draw a chord as a Pitch stack, a Pitch wheel or Voice lanes. In Missing tone, Scale degrees and Chords in context, the drone turns the answers into degrees of the key, and a cadence can set the key first.

The three views, the drone and the cadence are free.

Free practice, and eight weeks of it

Free practice holds any chord you pick and lets you bring each voice forward yourself, or play any mode at a level you have opened.

The Practice page shows this week's minutes, then time and right answers by mode over the last eight weeks.

Free for good. Pro takes each mode all the way.

Private by default

Earling runs on your device. There is no account, no analytics and no tracking. It asks for one permission, the microphone, and listens only while you sing: your voice is heard on the device as you sing and never recorded or sent. Your progress stays on your device. The privacy policy
