Privacy policy

Effective: on Earling 1.0's release · Applies to: Earling for Mac, iPad and iPhone

Earling collects nothing. It runs on your device, asks for one permission, the microphone, and listens only while you sing. This page says what the app uses, and what for.

What Earling collects

Nothing. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and Earling makes no network requests of its own. There is no server of ours to send anything to.

Your settings (the chord sound, the drone, the structure view, the theme, the app icon, whether you sing after each answer) are saved with the system’s standard preferences storage, inside the app’s own container on your device.

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles.
  • No recordings of your voice, on the device or anywhere else.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking.
  • No advertising and no third-party code that collects data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites, and nothing to sell or share.

Earling collects no personal information from anyone, children under 13 included.

The microphone

Earling asks for the microphone the first time you reach the Sing step, and uses it only for that step, to hear whether you hit the note. Your voice is measured for its pitch on your device as it arrives. It is not recorded, not saved to a file and not sent anywhere, and nothing listens once the step ends or while the app is in the background.

You can practice without it: if you don’t allow the microphone, Sing becomes a tap, and nothing else changes. Settings › Singing › Sing after each answer turns the step off, and then the microphone never opens.

Your progress

The levels you have passed, your voice range (the lowest and highest notes you chose in the one-time setup, as two note numbers) and your practice log (minutes and right answers by mode and day) are kept in the app’s own container on your device. They never leave it, and they are removed when you delete the app.

Permissions

The microphone, as above, and nothing else. Earling does not use the camera, Bluetooth, MIDI devices, your location, your contacts or your photos, and it reads no files.

Purchases

Earling Pro and its free 14-day trial are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or address. Earling learns only whether Pro is unlocked on your Apple Account.

Contact

Questions about privacy in Earling: fretlingapp@gmail.com. Earling’s instruments and recordings are open-source material that runs on your device and sends nothing; they are credited in the app’s About screen. If Earling’s data behavior ever changes, this policy changes before the app does.
