Free and Pro

The whole path is free to walk, and three modes are free all the way. Earling Pro takes the other five to their last level: bought once, owned forever, on every device you sign into.

On this page

  • What's free
  • What Pro adds
  • The 14-day trial
  • How unlocking works

What's free

The free app is not a trial, and nothing in it expires. It holds 25 of the 47 levels.

  • Higher or lower, Intervals and Chord quality, at every level: 20 levels.
  • The first level of Spotlight, Missing tone, Scales and modes, Scale degrees and Chords in context, so every mode on the path opens.
  • Hear, Check and Sing in every round, with the microphone or without it.
  • The Pitch stack, the Pitch wheel and Voice lanes; the drone and the cadence.
  • Free practice: the pad, and every free level.
  • The Practice page.

Nothing that is free today moves behind the unlock later.

What Pro adds

ModeWhat Pro opens
SpotlightLevels 2 to 12: less help, close and inverted chords, 7th chords.
Missing toneLevels 2 to 5: inverted chords, 7th chords, two tones missing.
Scales and modesLevels 2 to 3: the seven modes, harmonic and melodic minor.
Scale degreesLevels 2 to 3: every degree, in major and minor keys.
Chords in contextLevels 2 to 4: every chord of the key, inverted, and 7th chords.
Free practiceThe same levels, from its list.

The 14-day trial

Try all of Pro free for two weeks, from the button on the unlock sheet. Nothing starts on its own, nothing renews, and when it ends Earling returns to the free levels with your progress where you left it.

How unlocking works

  • One purchase, once. No subscription, and no tip jar.
  • Every device. The unlock covers every Mac, iPad and iPhone signed into your Apple Account, and supports Family Sharing.
  • Restore Purchases is on the unlock sheet, for a new device or a reinstall.
