Free and Pro

Everything you need to learn the neck is free, for good. Fretling Pro is the deep end on top — bought once, owned forever, on every device you sign into.

On this page

  • What's free
  • What Pro adds
  • How unlocking works
  • Where tips fit in

What's free

The free app is not a trial and nothing in it expires. It is the whole beginner story, and most of the intermediate one:

  • All three learning modes — Scales, Chords, Triads — and the sentence header.
  • The core scales: Major, Minor, both Pentatonics, and Blues.
  • The Chord Atlas with the chords of the key, Roman numerals, and previews.
  • Identifying a chord by tapping notes onto the neck.
  • Every overlay: CAGED, pentatonic positions, 3NPS, string sets, triad boxes — and chord shapes with finger numbers.
  • The marker display bar, tap to play, the scale timeline, play chips, and the fret range limiter.
  • The tuner's Fast mode with the Needle and Bar displays.
  • The complete metronome — full tempo range, every meter and subdivision, tap tempo, three click sounds. No caps.
  • The mixer, all six playback instruments, chord pad layers, and all fourteen app icons.

A promise worth stating plainly: nothing that is free today moves behind the unlock later.

Everything in this table is marked Pro where it appears elsewhere in the guide, so you can tell at a glance which side of the line a feature sits on.

Locked features stay visible where they live, dimmed with a small lock, so you always know what is there. A locked scale or setting opens one quiet paywall sheet when you tap it: what Pro holds, and the price. The three Pro practice tools — the amp, the looper and Live Detect — wear a lock badge on their icon and still open when tapped: the panel says what the tool does and offers an Unlock Fretling Pro button, so you meet the feature before you meet the price. Nothing counts down, nags, or interrupts on its own.

How unlocking works

  • One purchase, once. Fretling Pro is a single one-time App Store purchase — no subscription, nothing recurring.
  • Every device. The same purchase unlocks Fretling on Mac, iPad, and iPhone signed into your Apple Account, and it supports Family Sharing.
  • Your settings survive the lock. If a Pro setting is stored — say the tuner was on Precision — the app quietly uses the free equivalent until you unlock, then your stored choice comes straight back. Nothing is reset.
  • Restore anytime. The paywall's Restore Purchases re-checks your Apple Account on a new device or after a reinstall. The entry point lives at the bottom of the settings sidebar, under Fretling Pro.

Where tips fit in

The tip jar in About is unchanged: tips are optional, come in three sizes, and unlock nothing. They are a thank-you channel, not a paywall — every free feature is available whether or not you ever tip, and Pro is the only purchase that changes what the app does.
