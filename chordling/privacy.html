Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-14 · Applies to: Chordling for Mac, iPad and iPhone

The short version

Chordling runs on your device. It has no account, no analytics and no ads. Your songs stay on your device and, if you turn iCloud on, sync through your own iCloud account, which we cannot see.

What Chordling stores

  • Your songs: their melodies, chords, harmony parts and settings. They are stored on your device. When iCloud is on for Chordling, Apple syncs them through the private iCloud database of your own Apple Account, so they appear on your other devices. We have no access to that database.
  • Your preferences and Pitch Matching results: appearance, language, notation, playback and similar settings, and the scores of the Pitch Matching Test, stored in the system’s standard preferences storage inside the app’s own container. They never leave your device. On iPad and iPhone, deleting the app removes them; on a Mac they stay in the app’s container until you remove it.

The microphone

Chordling asks for the microphone for two features: Listen, which shows the pitch you sing on the piano roll, and the Pitch Matching Test. The sound is analyzed live, on your device, while one of them is on. It is not recorded, not stored and not sent anywhere. If you never use either feature, Chordling never asks.

Exports

Export Audio and Export Progression write a file only where you choose to save or share it. Chordling sends nothing anywhere by itself.

Purchases

Chordling Pro and its free trial are in-app purchases handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, name or address; Apple’s privacy policy governs the transaction.

What Chordling does not do

  • No accounts, sign-in or profiles
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting or usage tracking
  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data
  • No tracking across apps or websites, as Apple’s App Tracking Transparency defines it
  • No sale or sharing of personal information: there is none to sell or share

Children

Chordling collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13. It has no messaging, no sharing with other users and no way for anyone to contact a player through the app.

Third-party components

Chordling’s instruments come from two sound sets, FreePats and GeneralUser GS, and the band’s drums from Pearl Master Studio Pack 1 by enoe. All three play on your device and send nothing. They are credited in About.

Changes to this policy

If Chordling’s data behavior ever changes, this policy changes before that version ships, and the effective date above moves with it.
