Free & Pro

Writing, hearing and understanding are free. Chordling Pro adds more colors, more voices and the band. One purchase, no subscription.

As of 2026-10-14, for Chordling 1.0. What Pro holds may still change before launch; anything free stays free.

Free

  • As many songs as you like, synced through your own iCloud
  • The melody on its piano roll, with Tap keys, and the song’s form: intro, verse, chorus and the rest
  • The chord lane, the ten progression templates and the progression editor
  • Color bands and melody guides: the scale, chord tones, guide tones and targets, and Under the Melody
  • Harmonize in two styles, Simple and Sevenths, with a mix picked bar by bar and Harmonize again
  • Why it works: what each chord change does, in plain words
  • One harmony part, in any blend
  • Listen: see the pitch you sing on the melody lane
  • The instruments, the metronome and the tone controls (the sung Voice sounds are Pro)
  • Export Audio, and Export Progression to open your chords in Fretling

Chordling Pro

One purchase, on Mac, iPad and iPhone

  • More harmonizer styles: Colors, Dominants and Jazz, and the Pedal point, Modal and Minor color flavors
  • More voices: up to three harmony parts, four voices with the melody; sung parts; the Pitch Matching Test
  • The band: bass lines, drum grooves and style presets

Locked features stay in sight with a small lock. Tap one to see what Pro holds and its price; nothing locked ever opens by itself.

Try it free for 14 days

The paywall offers a 14-day trial of everything in Pro. You start it yourself, it is said in full before it starts, and it ends on its own: nothing is charged. Once it ends, your songs keep every choice you made, and they come back the moment you buy Pro.

Family Sharing and restoring

One purchase covers Mac, iPad and iPhone, and can be shared with your family through Family Sharing. On a new device, open any locked feature and tap Restore Purchases.

The price

The App Store shows the price in your currency before you buy. Apple handles the purchase; see the privacy policy.
