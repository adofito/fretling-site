Write a melody and hear the chords that fit it, and why.

Chordling is the two-way harmony app. Start from a tune and get its chords in many styles, each with its reason in plain words. Or start from the chords and build a melody over them. Then add harmony parts on top and hear it all back.

  • Mac, iPad and iPhone
  • Free, with a one-time Pro unlock
  • No account, no analytics
  • English and Spanish

Melody to chords

Write a tune on the piano roll and harmonize it in the style you choose. Hear each result against your song, keep the chords you like, harmonize again for the rest, and choose how often the chords change.

Chords to melody

Lay down a progression in the chord lane at the top or in the progression editor, then write over it while the app shows which notes fit each chord.

Harmony on top

Add a harmony part written from your melody, pick how it blends, and hear the whole song back with the band under it.

A reason for every chord

Other tools suggest chords. Chordling says what each change does and why, in plain words first and the theory term second, from its own analysis of your song. Nobody needs to read music or know theory to start.
